Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Who we are

Aidan Claffey Travel is a minibus and coach hire business based in Borrisokane, North Tipperary, Ireland. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The service you're enquiring about, preferred travel date, and number of passengers
  • Any details you include in your message

How we use your information

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to arrange and manage your booking. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Your enquiry is delivered to us using Formspree, a third-party form processing service, which handles form submissions and forwards them to our email inbox. Formspree processes this data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.

If we enable website analytics (such as Google Analytics), it will only run after you accept cookies via the banner on this site, and any data collected will be used solely to understand site traffic and improve the website.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting booking, and for a reasonable period afterward for our own record-keeping.

Cookies

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve your browser connecting to Google's servers. If you accept our cookie banner, we also load Google Analytics to help us understand site traffic; declining keeps this switched off.

Your rights

Under Irish and EU data protection law (GDPR), you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do so, email us at claffeyaidan50@gmail.com.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at claffeyaidan50@gmail.com or 087 653 1777.
