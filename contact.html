FAQs

Common questions

How quickly will I receive a quote?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within two business days, though we'll often get back to you sooner. For urgent bookings, please call us directly.

How far in advance should I book?

Wedding services like our night shuttle bus are often booked months in advance, so get in touch as early as you can. For other events, shorter notice is generally fine, though we can't always guarantee availability — just ask.

Are your vehicles insured and roadworthy?

Yes. Every vehicle in our fleet is fully licensed, insured, and maintained to current safety standards, with regular inspections.
