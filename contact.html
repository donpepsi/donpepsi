FAQs

Common questions

How quickly will I receive a quote?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day, often sooner. For urgent bookings, please call us directly.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, we recommend booking several weeks ahead. For smaller group hire, we can often accommodate shorter notice — just ask.

Are your vehicles insured and roadworthy?

Yes. Every vehicle in our fleet is fully licensed, insured, and maintained to current safety standards, with regular inspections.

Do you provide transport outside Ireland?

We primarily operate within Ireland but can discuss cross-border trips on request — get in touch with your itinerary and we'll advise.
